## Summary of the new model (PRD §5A)
- No faucet, no allowance, no buy-in. Participants hold a **deposit** (from the host in sponsored rooms, their own in pooled rooms).
- Opening a question charges one **play** (play price, set per room) from every participant in the room into that question's pot and mints an Answer token. Answering burns it; sitting out forfeits it into the pot.
- `resolve` mints a **Correct** token to each winner; the pot splits evenly among Correct holders.
- Unspent deposit is refunded at close. `leave` is permanent and refunds the rest.
- Sponsored (`[MVP]`, demoed) and pooled share the same contract path.
